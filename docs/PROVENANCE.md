# Provenance and rights review

The repository uses Apache-2.0 for its source, documentation, schemas, and examples;
`NOTICE` records project origin. Trademark permission is separate, as explained
in [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md). No trademark registration, legal entity,
exclusive copyright ownership, or patent clearance is implied.

## Review before distribution

A maintainer must review and retain an exact-revision record of:

- authority to license original contributions, including any employer/client terms;
- the origin and licenses of copied code, documentation, schemas, fixtures and assets;
- required third-party notices and compatibility of their terms;
- the intended public boundary and any removed private product/customer material;
- the actual rights holder and authority for any requested trademark permission.

The record may be private where it contains contracts, identities or customer
information. Publish required attribution and license notices, not confidential
contracts. A clean automated scan is not a copyright, trademark, or patent opinion.

The project declares no third-party Python runtime package dependencies in
`pyproject.toml`. Python, Git, Podman, optional provider CLIs, build/test tools and
GitHub Actions remain separately licensed dependencies of the chosen environment.
The absence of runtime dependencies does not prove that all source was originally
authored here. Recheck provenance whenever new material or assets are added.

## AI-assisted and third-party material

Review generated or copied material for provenance, attribution, secrets and
restrictions before merging. Do not represent model output as automatically
original or as an assignment of someone else's rights. Keep required upstream
notices with any redistributed material. Add a third-party notice inventory when
third-party material is actually included; do not invent an empty clearance claim.

## History and irreversible disclosure

Removing a file from the current tree does not remove old commits, PR discussions,
CI logs, caches or artifacts. A source snapshot or secret scanner covers only its
reported scope. Review retained historical product-integration material before
visibility changes. Do not rewrite history without separately considering pinned
consumer commits, open PRs and backups.

Do not publish an unreviewed source history just to obtain a successful public
clone test. Complete the private preparation first, then follow
[PUBLICATION](PUBLICATION.md). Possible patent filings or jurisdiction-specific
brand clearance must be resolved by the actual rights holder with qualified
advice before a disclosure that may affect their rights.
